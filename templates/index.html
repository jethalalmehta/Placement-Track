Core Architecture

Designed for Engineering Students

Everything required to systematically organize, test, and track your campus recruitment journey.

Preparation Tracker

Organize syllabus into Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Ability, Technical Subjects (OS, DBMS, CN), Coding Practice, and Interview Prep with completion tracking.

Practice Test Analytics

Log mock assessments with scores, calculate dynamic percentage averages, visualize performance trajectories over time, and isolate weak categories needing revision.

Companies & Drives

Catalog on-campus and off-campus recruitment opportunities with roles, salary packages (CTC), eligibility criteria, required skillsets, and application deadlines.

Application Progress & Analysis

Monitor every application through Applied, Shortlisted, Assessment/Test, Interview, Selected, and Not Selected with real-time conversion analytics.

Workflow

How It Works

A disciplined 3-step loop that turns scattered preparation into measurable placement readiness.

01

Map Your Curriculum

Add specific topics across core CSE subjects, data structures, and aptitude. Set target completion dates and priority levels to stay focused.

02

Record Practice Tests

Log mock test scores from platform assessments (TCS NQT, AMCAT, LeetCode, HackerRank). Review automatic average calculations and topic diagnostics.

03

Track Drives & Offers

Add campus recruitment drives with deadlines, transition your status across interview rounds, and analyze conversion rates until offer acceptance.
